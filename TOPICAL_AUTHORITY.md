# Topical Authority — toilet.cubicle.co.id

## Role and boundary

This domain is a specialist knowledge and commercial-support property for buyers, designers, contractors, facility teams, and owners planning toilet cubicle systems in Indonesia. It should help readers define requirements, compare configurations and materials, coordinate wet-area design, inspect installation, and maintain completed cubicles. Quotations, custom design, shop drawings, supply, installation, warranty decisions, and project-specific engineering remain on the commercial route or with the responsible manufacturer, contractor, designer, and authority.

## Context used

- Homepage H1: **Kontraktor Toilet Cubicle #1 Indonesia**. Visible sections emphasize four cubicle types, custom design, two material routes, testimonials, and contact.
- Product routes: `/type/premium`, `/type/single-door`, `/type/standar`, and `/type/two-tone`.
- Material routes: `/bahan/pvc` and `/bahan/phenolic`.
- `sitemap-complete.xml` contains 520 URLs: 488 location-swapped `cubicle-toilet-<lokasi>` routes plus the homepage, material/type hubs, and WordPress archive/API/cache noise.
- The repository contains 992 tracked files and 538 HTML files. Root HTML consists of the homepage plus 488 location templates; the remaining meaningful nested routes are two material and four type hubs.
- Material, structural, hardware, wet-area, fire/acoustic, hygiene, accessibility, warranty, and standards claims are evidence gates. They require the exact product/system, manufacturer data, current primary standards, relevant test reports, actual substrate/site conditions, and qualified project review before publication or use.
- Safety/standards relevance is a deep-mode signal, but compact evidence does not indicate a taxonomy conflict or substantial editorial corpus, so lean planning continues without inventing performance values or compliance claims.

## Internal-link rule

Setiap artikel menaut ke hub topiknya dan ke tahapan terdekat: brief → layout → material/configuration → survey/shop drawing → installation → maintenance. Artikel product/material menaut ke bukti yang harus diminta, artikel wet-area/hardware/structure menaut ke project verification, dan artikel standards/accessibility/safety selalu menyatakan batas general guidance serta kebutuhan reviewer berwenang. Rute komersial hanya menerima tautan setelah pembaca memiliki brief atau checklist yang cukup.
